MEMBER 1: TAB 1 — SEND & RECEIVE MONEY (EVERYDAY WALLETS & REFUNDS)
===========================================================================================

Step 1A: Send a Normal Payment (K125.00)
  What to Click:
    On Tab 1 ("1. Send & Receive Money"), keep "From Customer" as Alice (K1,000.00), "To Customer" as Bob (K500.00), "Amount" as 125.00, and click the black "Send Money" button.

  What Happens on Screen:
    Alice's balance updates to K875.00, Bob's balance updates to K625.00, a Payment Receipt appears in the middle, and two matching entries (-K125.00 Money Out and +K125.00 Money In) appear in the Customer Passbook table at the bottom.

  What to Say to the Lecturer:
    "Good day Sir. I am presenting Tab 1, which shows our everyday Mobile Money wallets in Zambian Kwacha. When Alice sends K125 to Bob, our system records two matching entries in the Customer Passbook — K125 out of Alice and K125 into Bob — so money can never vanish or be created out of thin air."


Step 1B: Try Spending More Money Than Available
  What to Click:
    Click the button labeled "3. Try Spending K999,999 (Not Enough Balance)".

  What Happens on Screen:
    The receipt displays "Payment Declined — Not Enough Money" and Alice's balance stays safe at K875.00.

  What to Say to the Lecturer:
    "If a customer tries to send K999,999 when they only have K875, the system blocks the transfer immediately so no wallet can ever go negative."


Step 1C: Refund a Previous Payment
  What to Click:
    On the right panel under "Refund a Previous Payment", select the K125.00 payment and click "Refund Selected Payment".

  What Happens on Screen:
    Alice returns to K1,000.00, Bob returns to K500.00, and two new official Refund entries appear in the Customer Passbook.

  What to Say to the Lecturer:
    "When a customer or merchant refunds a payment, we never delete old records from the passbook. Instead, we record a new official refund entry so the audit trail stays 100 percent complete."

MEMBER 2: TAB 2 — DOUBLE-CLICK PROTECTION (100 TAPS ON SLOW NETWORK)
===========================================================================================

Step 2A: Quick Double-Tap Preview on Tab 1
  What to Click:
    First click "1. Test Double-Tap Protection (Click Twice)" on Tab 1 to show a quick 2-tap example, then click the second tab at the top ("2. Double-Click Protection (100 Taps)").

Step 2B: Run the 100 Rapid Taps Simulation
  What to Click:
    On Tab 2, click the black button labeled "Simulate 100 Rapid Taps (K150.00)".

  What Happens on Screen:
    The three summary boxes show:
    - Charged Only Once: 1 Time (K150.00)
    - Duplicate Taps Blocked: 99 Blocked
    - Customer Money Saved: K14,850.00
    Below, Tap #1 turns black (Charged K150.00) and Taps #2 through #100 stay white (Duplicate Blocked — K0.00).

  What to Say to the Lecturer:
    "I am presenting Tab 2, which solves one of the biggest complaints in Mobile Money: being charged twice when the network is slow. Here we simulate a customer's phone retrying the same K150 payment 100 times in a row. Tap number 1 pays K150, and Taps 2 through 100 are all safely ignored, saving the customer K14,850 in accidental duplicate charges."

MEMBER 3: TAB 3 — CRASH REFUND & NO OVERDRAFT (NETWORK FAILURE & HIGH RUSH)
===========================================================================================

Step 3A: 3-Step Automatic Crash Refund (Left Panel)
  What to Click:
    Click the third tab ("3. Crash Refund & No Overdraft"), then click "Simulate Mid-Payment Crash & Auto-Refund" on the left panel.

  What Happens on Screen:
    Three visual step boxes show:
    - Step 1 moves K150.00 from Alice into temporary holding.
    - Step 2 experiences a Power/Network Failure before Bob receives the money.
    - Step 3 automatically returns the K150.00 to Alice so her balance stays K1,000.00.

  What to Say to the Lecturer:
    "I am presenting Tab 3. First, on the left, what happens if money leaves Alice's wallet and the power or network crashes before Bob receives it? As shown in these 3 steps, when Step 2 fails, Step 3 automatically refunds Alice's K150 immediately so her money is never left hanging."


Step 3B: 50 Simultaneous Payments Test (Right Panel)
  What to Click:
    On the right panel, click "Run 50 Simultaneous Payments Test".

  What Happens on Screen:
    A wallet with only K10.00 receives 50 simultaneous payment requests of K0.30 each:
    - Approved: 33 Payments (K9.90)
    - Declined (No Funds): 17 Declined
    - Final Balance: K0.10 (Never Negative)

  What to Say to the Lecturer:
    "Second, on the right, we test what happens if a wallet only has K10.00 and 50 payments of 30 Ngwee arrive at the exact same instant. Our system lines them up one by one: 33 payments succeed for K9.90, 17 are declined because only 10 Ngwee is left, and the wallet never drops below zero."

  What to Say to the Lecturer:
    "I am presenting Tab 4, which protects our Mobile Money records against internal fraud or hackers. Every payment in the passbook is locked with a digital security seal linked to the payment before it. When we click this button to simulate someone secretly changing K250 to K999,999, the digital seal breaks immediately, raises a security alarm, and freezes the account."

  What to Click:
    Click "2. Restore Original Safe Records" to return the passbook to "ALL RECORDS VERIFIED & SAFE".
